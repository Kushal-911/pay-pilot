## Research limitations

- No private, “hidden,” employee-only, or internal company materials were accessed. These are not available through public browsing; conclusions are not based on them.
- LinkedIn/X search is incomplete and may require login. Results are used only when publicly viewable; no scraping or access-control circumvention was attempted.
- Search indexing finds some current Devpost projects even while the full gallery is unpublished. Search results are not exhaustive and may omit or misclassify projects.
- PayPal's internal system architecture and team intentions are not inferred beyond what official documentation explicitly states.
- The project metrics and merchant pain hypotheses need interviews/measurement; no first-party user research was conducted for this dossier.
